Support

VOCALOID Connections is maintained by sodapines as a small fan-made music puzzle project.

For bugs, song corrections, or suggestions, email kzen@sodapines.dev.

Hosting costs a bit each month to keep running. If you have enjoyed the game and want to chip in, even a one-time tip helps cover those costs.

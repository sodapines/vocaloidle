[ How to Play ]
1Each square needs a song matching both its row and column.
2Tap a square, type a song title, pick it from the list.
3Wrong answers cost a strike — you have 6. No song can be used twice.
4Rarer answers score more. Obvious picks score 0, deep cuts score up to 100 per square.
